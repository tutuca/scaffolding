# User — setup

## Getting an account

Accounts are not self-serve: the tenant's owner adds you as a member and grants you
a role. They will also set your active tenant.

## Signing in

1. Browse to the application (e.g. `http://localhost:8000`).
2. Sign in at `/login/` with your email and password.
3. You land on the home page, which shows the tenant you are looking at.

## Switching tenants

If you are a member of more than one tenant, an administrator sets your active
tenant; switching is done from the admin. The home page and the notes page always
act on your active tenant's rows.
